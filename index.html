ABOUT ME

Hi, I’m Stelios, founder and developer of Legend Knight Games, based in Athens, Greece. I specialize in Unity and C#, with experience in Game Development, Game Design, 3D Modeling, Pixel Art, and Video Editing. My focus is on creating engaging games, development tools, and interactive experiences while continuously expanding my skills across multiple disciplines. Passionate about video games since childhood, I’m dedicated to building creative projects and growing as an independent developer.
